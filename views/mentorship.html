How the 6-Month Matching Cycle Works

01
Intake & Diagnostic
Submit your current career stage, 3 specific technical or leadership goals, and preferred timezone.
02
Curated Matching
ATS committee and algorithm pair you with a compatible alumni mentor based on trajectory and track.
03
Introductory Sync
A 45-minute chemistry check to establish meeting frequency, NDA terms, and milestones.
04
Milestone Check-ins
Bi-weekly 1:1 sessions supplemented with ATS quarterly review sheets and capstone evaluations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ATS Mentorship

What is the expected time commitment for mentors and mentees?
Mentors and mentees typically meet for 45 to 60 minutes every two weeks over a 6-month cycle. In total, expect approximately 2 to 3 hours per month including brief prep and milestone check-ins.
Is there any fee to join the mentorship program?
No. The ATS Mentorship Initiative is entirely free of charge and fully sponsored by the Alumni Tracking System and University Alumni Endowment.
Can alumni located outside the United States participate?
Yes, absolutely. Over 40% of our alumni mentors and mentees reside across Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and the Middle East. Sessions are conducted virtually via Google Meet or Zoom.
Can I switch mentors if the alignment does not fit?
Yes. After the initial 30-day trial period, either party may request a rematch with zero friction through the ATS committee coordinator.
